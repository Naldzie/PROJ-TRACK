===============================================================================
  DECISIONS MADE DURING IMPLEMENTATION
===============================================================================

  1. Polynomial method checks regression_method === 'polynomial':
     The estimatePolynomial() method returns null when the regression
     method is not polynomial, because it needs cubic coefficients.
     This means on typical linear/SLR data, the polynomial slot shows "N/A".
     This is correct behavior — the method only activates when polynomial
     regression was selected by the model selection logic.

  2. Blade card placement:
     The multi-method card is placed AFTER the timeline card but WITHIN the
     collapsible "Detailed Analysis" section, rather than between the status
     card and the collapsible as the plan originally suggested. This keeps
     the initial view clean while still showing the comparison to users who
     expand details.
