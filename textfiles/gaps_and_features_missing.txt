  CORE PM FEATURES

  20. [DONE] RISK REGISTER
      - A dedicated risk tracker distinct from the existing Issues feature
      - Risks are PROSPECTIVE (what could go wrong); Issues are RETROSPECTIVE
        (what has already happened)
      - Fields: risk description, probability (Low/Medium/High), impact
        (Low/Medium/High), risk score (probability × impact), mitigation plan,
        status (Open/Mitigated/Closed), date identified
      - Why it matters: Standard PMU feature. The existing Issues system
        captures problems after they occur; a risk register captures them
        before they happen, enabling proactive management
      - IMPLEMENTED (Aug 6, 2026): branch phase-20-risk-register, 4 phases.
        Lives in the Issues & Risks tab (All/Issues/Risks toggle). Admin/Staff
        CRUD, contractor read-only. Auto score 1-9 + Low/Med/High level.
        ActivityLog on every change. High-level risks notify admins + the
        project's contractor (RiskReported). 15 feature tests; full suite 351
        passing. Plan: textfiles/risk_register_plan.txt
      - ENHANCED (Aug 6, 2026): RISK → ISSUE CONVERSION + parity upgrades.
        Convert-to-Issue button on Open/Mitigated risks opens a pre-filled,
        editable modal (name/description/type/severity/baseline; status fixed
        Open). Conversion creates the issue, links both records
        (risk.converted_to_issue_id ↔ issue.source_risk_id), marks the risk
        Realized, transfers + relabels risk evidence to the issue, audits
        'Convert Risk to Issue', and notifies admins + contractor. Risks gained
        an optional baseline month link and evidence attachments (context
        'risk') like issues. Delete guards: Realized risks are blocked from
        delete (422 "delete the issue first"); deleting a converted issue
        reverts its risk Realized → Open so it is preserved. Realized risks get
        a badge + "→ Issue #N" link; converted issues get a "from Risk" badge.
        RiskRegisterTest now 30 tests; full suite 367 passing. Plan:
        textfiles/risk_to_issue_conversion_plan.txt
