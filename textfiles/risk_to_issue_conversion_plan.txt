==============================================================================
  SECTION 3 — PROBLEM STATEMENT
==============================================================================

  Risks (prospective: "what could go wrong") and Issues (retrospective:
  "what already went wrong") are stored separately with NO link between them.

  When a risk actually materializes, the user must:
    1. manually create an issue from scratch, re-typing the name/description,
    2. leave the original risk "Open" forever with no record that it came true,
    3. re-enter the baseline month, severity, etc. by hand.

  Consequences:
    • No audit trail connecting a materialized risk to the issue it caused.
    • Open-risk counts stay inflated after a risk becomes real.
    • Evidence attached to a risk (site photos, quotes, permits) cannot follow
      the problem into its new life as an issue.
    • Risks cannot be tied to a specific baseline month target, unlike issues.

==============================================================================
  SECTION 4 — CHOSEN DIRECTION
==============================================================================

  Add a one-way conversion path risk → issue, plus two parity upgrades so
  risks behave like issues where it makes sense:

  1. Conversion button ("Convert to Issue")
     Admin/Staff click a button on a non-Realized risk → a pre-filled,
     editable modal → creates an Issue, marks the risk Realized, and links the
     two records (risk.converted_to_issue_id ↔ issue.source_risk_id).

  2. Baseline link on risks (parity with issues)
     Optional baseline_schedule_id on risks so a risk can threaten a specific
     month/phase target; carries over automatically on conversion.

  3. Attachments on risks (parity with issues)
     Optional attachments using a NEW context label 'risk' (evidence the risk
     is credible). Reuses the existing AttachmentController + policy — no new
     attachment routes.

  4. Attachment behavior on conversion
     Risk attachments TRANSFER to the new issue and are relabeled context
     'issue' so they render as normal issue evidence in the issues tab.

  5. Delete-link guards (edge cases — decided with user)
     • Reverse (delete a Realized risk): BLOCKED. destroy() rejects a risk
       with converted_to_issue_id set — "delete the issue first". Nothing
       can dangle.
     • Forward (delete a converted issue): risk reverts Realized → Open and
       converted_to_issue_id is cleared, so the risk is preserved and can be
       re-converted or mitigated. The issue's attachments (transferred risk
       evidence) are deleted with the issue, as with any issue today.

  Rationale: reuses proven Issues patterns (controller shape, modal-driven
  forms, polymorphic attachments, notification wiring) and keeps the audit
  trail intact in both deletion directions.

==============================================================================
  SECTION 8 — DECISION LOG
==============================================================================

  Decision 1 — One-way conversion risk → issue (with link)
    Context: user asked whether a materialized risk should become an issue.
    Options: manual re-entry; auto-link on conversion.
    Decision: Convert to Issue button (admin/staff) creates the issue
      pre-filled, sets risk Realized, links both directions.
    Consequences: audit trail; open-risk counts drop when a risk materializes.

  Decision 2 — Baseline link on risks
    Context: user asked whether risks should be tied to a baseline like issues.
    Decision: add optional baseline_schedule_id to risks; carries over on
      conversion.
    Rationale: risk can threaten a specific month/phase target.

  Decision 3 — Risk attachments + context label
    Context: user asked whether risks should have attachments like issues.
    Decision: yes — single new context 'risk' (evidence the risk is credible).
      Mitigation plan remains a text field; attachments are proof.
    Consequences: Attachment::CONTEXTS += 'risk';
      ALLOWED_ATTACHABLE_TYPES += Risk::class. Reuses existing upload/view
      routes + policy.

  Decision 4 — Attachment behavior on conversion
    Context: what happens to risk attachments when the risk becomes an issue.
    Decision: transfer to the issue and relabel context 'risk' → 'issue' so
      they render as normal issue evidence.
    Rationale: evidence follows the problem into its new lifecycle.

  Decision 5 — Reverse delete (Realized risk): BLOCKED
    Context: user asked about the reverse edge case.
    Decision: destroy() rejects a risk with converted_to_issue_id set.
    Rationale: prevents dangling issue.source_risk_id; protects audit trail.

  Decision 6 — Forward delete (converted issue): revert risk to Open
    Context: symmetric edge case.
    Decision: deleting the converted issue clears converted_to_issue_id and
      sets risk status back to Open (risk preserved, re-convertable).
    Rationale: don't lose a risk because its issue was removed.

  Decision 7 — Severity mapping on conversion
    Decision: risk level → issue severity (Low→Low, Medium→Medium, High→High);
      issue_type defaults to 'other'. Editable in the convert modal.

  Decision 8 — Branching
    Decision: work continues on branch phase-20-risk-register; merge to main
      only after user review/approval (Rule 12).
